Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I send a mushroom invitation?
A: Use the "Mushroom Invitation" feature inside the app to create and share your invitation.
Q: How does postcard exchange work?
A: You can connect with other players and exchange postcards directly through the app.
Q: The app is not working properly
A: Please try restarting the app. If the issue persists, contact us via email.
