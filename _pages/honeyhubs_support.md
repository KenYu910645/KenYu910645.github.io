## 📣 Support & Campaign

Thank you for your interest in **HoneyHubs**! This page is the central hub for our campaign, updates, and ways you can support the project.

### What is HoneyHubs?

**HoneyHubs** is a Pikmin-themed platform app designed for fans of the Pikmin universe. It lets you:

- Manage your Pikmin squads and track their progress
- Discover and explore hidden hubs across different environments
- Connect with a community of fellow Pikmin enthusiasts
- Track your in-game achievements and milestones
